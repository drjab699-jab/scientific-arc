## What this is

One arc: the Big Bang to atoms to molecules to cellular organelles to the cell. At each stage the same events are described three ways — at the chemical layer, the biological layer, and the informational genetic layer.

The four characters a sequencer prints are not a fact about chemistry. They are a fact about what a laboratory could purify in 1953. This volume starts one level below them, at the atoms, and builds up. The unit throughout is the nucleotide.

## The thread that runs its length

Sulfur has a partner, and the partnership is the plot. Oxygen sits directly above sulfur in the periodic table — same column, same outer-shell arithmetic, same two bonds. They are conjugate: interchangeable in the architecture, never interchangeable in effect. Every place biology uses one, substituting the other changes what happens.

Their behavior is opposite in one crucial respect. Oxygen occupies one state and takes electrons out of circulation; sulfur occupies eight and passes them along. Oxygen is where flows end. Sulfur is how they travel.

That opposition produced three things the volume follows from beginning to end. It produced DNA, when a sulfur atom removed an oxygen from a sugar. It produced the Great Oxidation Event, which is dated not by measuring oxygen but by watching a sulfur isotope signal disappear from the rock. And it produced inosine — because inosine is what happens when an amine leaves adenine and an oxygen takes its place.

Sulfur is the protagonist. Oxygen is the antagonist that shares its face. Inosine is what oxygen leaves behind on the purine, and the cell took that piece of damage and made it into an instrument for reading the code.

## How it was built

Before there was a page of this volume there was a wall. Nine feet across, eight feet high, and covered edge to edge with hand-lettered cards, printed figures, photographs, pathway charts and arrows drawn between them, begun in 1997.

It was not designed. It was accumulated:

- one verified item at a time
- nothing went up until it had been checked against a source
- placed next to what it touched, because position on the wall records relationship, not decoration
- arrows added when a connection held, and left off when it did not
- nothing removed to make the pattern neater — the contradictions stayed up where they could be seen

The book was built the same way, over the same years, by the same rule. A source is read. A claim is extracted. The claim is checked against the primary literature. It survives, or it does not. What survives is written down, with the condition that would refute it. The next item arrives and must live beside it.

That method produces a particular kind of object. It is long, because the evidence is long. It has no thesis chapter, because the thesis is what remained standing. And it can be entered at any section, because each one was built to stand on its own before it was placed.

## How to read it

Do not start at page one and go forward. Enter wherever your subject is and follow the links.

- **Contents** — every one of the 2,680 sections, by division and page
- **Index** — the unified index across both books
- **Colors** — the color index; the color convention marks an element and nothing else, mechanically, wherever it occurs, which turns a typographic rule into a measurement

Every page footer carries all three as links. Every Part ends by stating the conditions under which it would be wrong.

## The plates
